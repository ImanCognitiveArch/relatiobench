# Community code of conduct

We welcome people across disciplines, backgrounds and levels of experience.
Discuss code, evidence and ideas respectfully. Give actionable feedback, credit
others' work, and make room for questions and disagreement.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized targeting, and repeated
unwanted contact are not acceptable. Do not post personal information, clinical
records or credentials.

This policy covers project issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces
maintained for RelatioBench. It does not claim authority over unrelated spaces.

For concerns, use an available private GitHub reporting route. If none is
available, request a private contact channel without posting sensitive details.
Project maintainer Iman Khorami reviews reports and may remove content, limit
participation or block accounts, proportionate to the conduct. Request review
of a moderation decision through the same private channel when available.
No response-time or confidentiality guarantee is promised.
